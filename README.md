## Timothy Pittaway

Lecturer and researcher in Agriculture and Business Management at SRUC, Aberdeen.

My research looks at **transitional vulnerability**, which is how farms, rural businesses and food supply chains become exposed while they are going through change. I work in a participatory way, alongside the communities and businesses I study.

### Research areas

- Agri-food system resilience
- Rural entrepreneurship
- Supply chain vulnerability
- Qualitative research methods and CAQDAS (NVivo, ATLAS.ti)

### Research data

- [NBS treescapes reverse-logic analysis](https://github.com/timothypittaway/nbs-treescapes-reverse-logic), the data, search strategy and framework behind my 2026 IJERPH article on nature-based solution treescapes across Europe ([doi:10.3390/ijerph23060691](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph23060691))
